## Audit report: nix-bitcoin fork @ ebf269e

**Surface mapped:** 24 NixOS modules (bitcoind, lnd, lndconnect, electrs, btcpayserver/nbxplorer, netns-isolation, secrets, operator, onion-addresses/services, nodeinfo, backups, security, 6 presets, krops/nixops deployment), the C setcap helper `netns-exec`, packaging (`btcpayserver` 2.4.4 override, `nbxplorer`, `lndinit`, `krops`, patched `nixops`), helpers (`makeShell.nix`/`update-nix-bitcoin`, `fetch-release`, `update-flake.sh`), and CI (`cve-scan.sh`, `release-tag.yml`).

**Verified clean (investigated and dismissed):** `netns-exec` deployment (owner-only 0500, `cap_sys_admin=ep` file cap, absolute-path `execv`, caps dropped pre-exec, whitelist matches shipped services); `setup-secrets` (scoped root sandbox, dotglob lockdown, all interpolations `escapeShellArg`d); `onion-addresses` (symlink refusal, single-read onion-regex validation, build-as-root-then-chown ordering); lnd macaroon pipeline (admin macaroon read via `runuser` as lnd, 64 KiB response cap, root-only staging on same tmpfs, `mv -fT`); rpcauth password via file not argv; wireguard preset (`-i wg-nb` + peer `/32` accept, REJECT inserted at top of `nixos-fw`/`FORWARD`, assertions refusing disabled firewall and the nftables backend); no secrets in `/nix/store`; all fetches hash-pinned; version-guarded btcpayserver override; CVE scan fails closed on malformed reports; commits SSH-signed (verified `gpgsig` header present on HEAD).

No high or medium findings. The tree has visibly absorbed three prior audit rounds; what remains is two lows and three informational items, most already documented by the maintainers.

**Bottom line:** ship-shape tree. F4/F5 are known, documented, and correctly guarded.
