## Missed / misread (per Astra)

- [medium] **Macaroon publication follows destination directory symlinks** — modules/lnd.nix:301
  The lnd user owns /run/lnd and can replace the macaroon destination with a symlink to another directory. GNU mv -f treats that destination as a directory, letting the root helper place attacker-controlled macaroon bytes there under the configured basename, contrary to the atomic-publication comment. Use mv -fT -- so the destination is always treated as the exact filename.
- [low] **Report-processing errors become clean scan results** — test/ci/cve-scan.sh:68
  The array-only validation accepts malformed entries such as [{"affected_by":true}], which then make the counting jq expression fail. The '|| echo 0' fallback converts that error into found=false, allowing the workflow to close the tracking issue; fail the scan on processing errors and validate entry fields.
