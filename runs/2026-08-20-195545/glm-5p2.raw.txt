## Audit summary

**Commit audited:** `76f3b120f8aa6fb1af125b41845b3b9f9c638515` (fork of nix-bitcoin, trimmed to bitcoind, lnd, electrs, btcpayserver/nbxplorer + support infra).

**Surface map.** Funds/keys reachable in: `bitcoind.nix` (wallet, RPC cookie, RPC users), `lnd.nix` (wallet seed/password, admin macaroon, TLS key), `btcpayserver.nix` (lnd macaroon, RPC creds), `secrets/secrets.nix` (all secret perms), `onion-addresses.nix` (root + DAC_OVERRIDE copier), `netns-exec` (cap_sys_admin wrapper), `operator.nix` (doas/sudo rules). I focused there.

**What I checked and cleared (with evidence):**
- **Secrets in store / argv:** No `builtins.readFile`/`fileContents` on secrets anywhere. `lnd.conf` (writeText) and nbxplorer config hold no secrets; passwords are appended at runtime. rpcauth.py takes the password via file, not argv (L-6 fix). lndinit reads seed/password from files. No `environment=` with secret values.
- **Macaroon symlink race:** `lnd.nix` ExecStartPost stages macaroons in a root-only `mktemp -d` (0700) on the same tmpfs as `/run/lnd`, then `mv -f` (rename, atomic, doesn't follow a planted symlink). Solid (b2eb47f, 9b71bec).
- **Onion-addresses symlink privesc:** refuses symlinked hostname files, refuses symlinked dataDir, builds per-user dirs as root:root 0700 and chowns last, `chown -h` everywhere. Solid.
- **RPC whitelist:** public + btcpayserver users share a whitelist with no wallet/send/stop/import/DoS calls; `sendrawtransaction`/`signrawtransactionwithkey` only relay/with-caller-provided-keys. privileged user (full access) and its HMAC/password are bitcoin-group-only; no service is in `bitcoin`. Cookie (full RPC) is bitcoin-group-only → just the operator.
- **Sandboxing:** `defaultHardening` applied to all funds services; only documented necessary weakenings (`.NET`/node JIT `MemoryDenyWriteExecute=false`; onion-addresses DAC_OVERRIDE as root). No silent `mkDefault` overrides dropping hardening.
- **Privilege boundaries:** lnd/electrs/nbxplorer share only `bitcoinrpc-public` (whitelisted user). btcpayserver's lnd macaroon is read-only + `invoices/address:write` (no `onchain/offchain:write`) → a compromised web app cannot send funds. No service can reach another's keys.
- **Ordering:** funds services `after nix-bitcoin-secrets.target`; wants-vs-requires changes are availability fixes that preserve boot ordering.
- **Supply chain:** nixpkgs pinned via flake.lock narHash; lndinit/nbxplorer pinned with hashes; fetch-release refuses upstream; krops pins commit; fetch-node-modules is FOD.
- **Network exposure:** services default to 127.0.0.1; lndconnect no longer forces 0.0.0.0 (M-2); wireguard's 0.0.0.0 is explicit + firewall-gated.

The fork carries the fixes from a prior audit (L-2/L-6/L-7/L-8/L-10/L-11, M-2/M-3) plus the recent macaroon and onion hardening. It is consistently hardened.
