Empirically confirmed the verification bypass. Audit complete. Summary below, findings as JSON at the end.

## Surface map

- **Modules** (`modules/`): bitcoind, lnd, electrs, btcpayserver/nbxplorer, lndconnect, wireguard preset, secrets system, operator/doas, onion services/addresses, netns-isolation, backups (duplicity), hardened presets, nodeinfo, krops/nixops deployment.
- **Packages** (`pkgs/`): btcpayserver 2.4.4 override with regenerated NuGet lockfile, nbxplorer 2.6.10, lndinit 0.1.36-beta, netns-exec (C, cap_sys_admin wrapper), pinned.nix/nixpkgs-pinned.nix.
- **Helpers/CI**: `helper/fetch-release` (GPG-verified upstream release fetcher), `makeShell.nix` (krops deploy shell), `update-flake.sh`, workflows (test, release-tag, cve-scan), `cve-whitelist.toml`.

## What was verified clean

- All fixed-output hashes against live sources: btcpayserver v2.4.4 src, nbxplorer v2.6.10 src, lndinit v0.1.36-beta src, rpcauth.py, both flake.lock nixpkgs revs (rev ↔ narHash match, official NixOS/nixpkgs).
- NuGet lockfile: spot-checked NBitcoin 10.0.10, HtmlSanitizer 9.2.1039, NBitcoin.Altcoins, Newtonsoft.Json 13.0.4, BouncyCastle against nuget.org (all match); fork vs nixpkgs deps delta is purely 2.4.2→2.4.4 version bumps, no smuggled packages.
- `helper/key-jonasnick.bin` contains exactly the documented fingerprint 36C7…8DCD0366.
- The hardening commits are genuine: lnd macaroon staging (root-only dir + rename + `mv -fT`), admin.macaroon read de-privileged via `runuser`, onion-hostname symlink/content validation, secrets-dir lockdown with dotglob, RPC whitelist trims, cookie 0640, wireguard firewall assertions, cve-scan fail-closed logic — all hold up under review.
- netns-exec: allowlist fail-closed, absolute-path exec, capabilities dropped after setns, wrapper mode 500 root:root owned by operator.
- Regtest of the HEAD commit's wireguard assertion changes: they tighten, not loosen.
