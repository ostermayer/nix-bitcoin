## Highest-value investigation: the tip commit's assertions

d5ac2b8 claims to close the "silent rule loss" class for the wireguard preset (its whole security model: lnd admin REST on 0.0.0.0 guarded only by iptables `extraCommands`). The two new assertions cover `networking.firewall.enable = false`, `networking.nftables.enable`, and `services.firewalld.enable`. However, nixpkgs 26.05 exposes `networking.firewall.backend` (enum iptables/nftables/firewalld) as a first-class user option. Setting `backend = "firewalld"` evades every assertion; the iptables firewall unit (the only consumer of `extraCommands`) is gated on `backend == "iptables"` and never exists, and the backend option does not enable the firewalld service — nothing filters anything. Verified by direct evaluation (above). The `backend = "nftables"` variant fails closed only by accident (nixpkgs' own `extraCommands == ""` assertion), not by the preset's design.

Secondary targets checked and clean: nbxplorer WorkingDirectory/content-root fix (test-pinned), lnd `wants`-vs-`requires`, `lndconnect-wg` flag plumbing (PATH-resolves the hiPrio wrapper; REST port matches the accept rule), wgConnect secrets ownership, setup-secrets sandbox (CAP set, ReadWritePaths, AF_UNIX-only), netns matrix, RPC whitelist contents vs. the documented safety bar, release-tag closure filter.
