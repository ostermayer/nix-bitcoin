Audit complete. Surface mapped, high-value targets investigated. Summary of what I checked, then findings.

## Surface map

**Fork delta**: ~60 fork commits on top of upstream fort-nix/nix-bitcoin, trimmed to bitcoind, lnd, electrs, btcpayserver/nbxplorer, lndconnect, wireguard preset, backups, netns-isolation. Prior audit history (docs/security-audit-2026-08-14.md + follow-up commits through HEAD 6181cd1) already fixed: clightning-rest 0.0.0.0 bind, lndconnect rebind, RPC whitelist DoS surface, onion-addresses symlink privesc, netns-exec PATH/exec issues, and (at HEAD) the root-read of admin.macaroon.

**Trust-boundary code paths examined**:
- Root-executing scripts: lnd macaroon `ExecStartPost` (unsandboxed `+` script), onion-addresses oneshot (root + DAC caps), setup-secrets. All now symlink-hardened with root-staged atomic renames; verified.
- `netns-exec` (pkgs/netns-exec/src/main.c): file-cap `cap_sys_admin=ep`, owner-only 500, netns whitelist, absolute-path execv, caps dropped before exec. Clean.
- Secret flow: generate-secrets (root, umask 0600), rpcauth wrapped to keep the password out of argv, per-secret chown/chmod. secretsDir is root-owned, so the chmod-follows-symlink on unprocessed files is not unprivileged-exploitable.
- Supply chain: flake.lock pins, hash-locked fetchers everywhere, btcpayserver 2.4.3 override is version-guarded; spot-checked the regenerated nuget lockfile (HtmlSanitizer 9.2.995, 166 packages, no anomalies).
- CI: `pull_request` runs receive no secrets (fork-PR signing-key exfil not possible); cve-scan fails closed on build/vulnix/JSON errors; whitelist is mostly per-CVE scoped.
- Pinned nixpkgs b18a4b90 postgresql defaults to `local all all peer`, so the btcpaydb/nbxplorer lateral-movement-via-trust-auth path does not exist.
