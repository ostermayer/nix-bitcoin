## What was verified

This fork carries an unusual density of prior adversarial audits (2026-08-14 through 2026-10-04, multi-model, with closure commits). I verified the claimed closures rather than re-trusting them:

- lnd macaroon path: root-only staging dir on the same tmpfs, atomic `mv -fT`, admin.macaroon read as the lnd user, 64 KiB response cap, `jq -ce ... | strings` validation, umask empirically confirmed 0640. Sound.
- onion-addresses: symlink-refusing reads, single-read TOCTOU closure via strict v3-onion content validation, root-built dirs chowned last, `chown -h`. Sound.
- netns-exec: absolute-path `execv`, cap return checks, exec-failure exit, whitelist matches shipped netns. Sound.
- wireguard preset: firewall-enable and effective-backend assertions cover both silent rule-loss paths; REST accept is interface-and-peer-scoped (cryptokey-routed, so source spoofing is excluded); restrictPeer REJECT inserted at chain top so it precedes standard accepts. Sound.
- fetch-release: gated by value on `NIX_BITCOIN_FETCH_UPSTREAM=1`, gpg verify bound to the data file, fingerprint pinned. Sound.
- cve-scan: fails closed on bad exit codes, non-JSON, malformed entries, non-numeric counts. Sound.
- All source fetches hash-pinned (flake.lock narHash, fetchFromGitHub hashes, fetchgit commit pin for krops, pinned rpcauth.py). No secrets in the store; conf files with credentials are datadir copies at 0600/0640.

Residual exposures are documented and consciously accepted in-repo: the wireguard REST 0.0.0.0 bind (tracked in docs/wireguard-rest-bind.md with a pre-flight checklist), the `.cookie` 0640 group read (bitcoind.nix L-10 note), operator's doas-as-lnd power (design).
