## Surface map

**Trust boundaries audited:** secrets lifecycle (`modules/secrets/secrets.nix`, deployment/{krops,nixops}.nix, `helper/makeShell.nix`); root-context scripts touching service-controlled paths (lnd macaroon `ExecStartPost`, `onion-addresses`, `setup-secrets`); privilege wrappers (`pkgs/netns-exec` C source + `security.wrappers`); network exposure (bitcoind/lnd/electrs/nbxplorer/btcpayserver modules, `netns-isolation`, onion services, wireguard/enable-tor/secure-node presets, RPC whitelist); supply chain (`helper/fetch-release`, `update-flake.sh`, `pkgs/overrides.nix`, `pkgs/krops`, flake.lock, `update-nix-bitcoin`); CI (`.github/workflows/*`, `test/ci/cve-scan.sh`, `cve-whitelist.toml`); dev/test/examples.

**Method:** full read of every fork-touched security-relevant file at `c021a3e`, plus `git diff v0.0.99..HEAD` to isolate fork deltas from upstream fort-nix behavior, and re-verification of every prior-audit fix the commit log claims (they hold).

**Verified good at this commit:** the lnd macaroon pipeline (root-only `mktemp` staging on the same tmpfs, admin macaroon read via `runuser -u lnd`, 64 KiB response cap, `mv -fT`); `onion-addresses` (symlink refusal, single-v3-onion content validation, root-populate-then-chown, `chown -h`); `setup-secrets` scoped-root sandbox; `netns-exec` (owner-only `0500`, `cap_sys_admin=ep`, absolute-path requirement, caps dropped before exec); `fetch-release` refusing upstream; krops pinned by commit; btcpayserver 2.4.3 version-guarded override with hash; SHA-pinned CI actions; fail-closed CVE scan; no secrets in the nix store; all fetches hash-pinned.
